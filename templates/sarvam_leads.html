Understanding the Lead Pipeline

Total Inquiries vs. Pipeline Stages:
Every voice call processed by the AI bot on a selected date enters this dashboard. The top summary cards show call outcomes, while the Kanban board organizes prospects into an active sales CRM pipeline.
1. All Leads: Complete repository of all inquiries and call records processed by the voice AI bot.
2. Interested: Prospects with confirmed buying intent, pricing requests, product inquiries, or store visits.
3. Callback: Prospects who specifically requested a follow-up callback. Track scheduled date/times and notes.
4. Closed: Prospects who completed or closed their inquiry, finalized bookings, or opted out.
